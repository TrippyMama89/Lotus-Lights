How to play

Make every petal bloom. A move changes the selected petal and all its directly neighboring petals. Level 1 uses a branching arrangement: the two side petals do not neighbor each other.

Tap once to preview which petals will change, then tap the same petal again to confirm. Undo and Restart are unlimited. Solved levels advance automatically.

These first ten levels are an engine test. The artwork and advanced ring rules will be added after we confirm the gameplay.
